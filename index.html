Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your documents never leave your device.
Is PressPixel really free?
Yes. No sign-up, no watermark, no file-size paywall, no daily limit.
Will compressing a PDF reduce its quality?
The Extreme, Recommended and Less levels convert pages to images, which can make text unselectable. The Light level keeps text selectable with a smaller size reduction.
Can I process password-protected PDFs?
Not yet. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then try again.
Does OCR work offline?
OCR downloads a language model the first time you use it, then works locally. It supports English, Hindi, Bengali, Spanish, French and German.
